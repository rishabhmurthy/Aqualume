# Privacy Policy for Aqualume

Aqualume respects your privacy. This app is designed for users of all ages.

## Information We Collect
Aqualume may collect the following data:
Location data (used only for app functionality)
Anonymous technical data (e.g., crash logs, performance data)
We do not collect personal details such as name, email, contacts, photos, or files.

## How We Use Location Data
Location information is used only to provide or improve app features.
It is not used for tracking or advertising.

## Data Sharing
Aqualume does not sell or share user data with third parties, except for essential services that help the app function (e.g., Google Play Services).

## Children’s Privacy
Aqualume is safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
Location data collected is used only for core app features.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will appear on this page.
